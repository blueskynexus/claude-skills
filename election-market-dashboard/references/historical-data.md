### Annualized returns by composition (all years, not just post-election)

| Composition | Annualized return | Source |
|-------------|------------------|--------|
| Divided government | 8.73% | Janus Henderson |
| Unified government | 9.24% | Janus Henderson |

Key finding: the difference is minimal. Markets price in the resolution of
uncertainty, not the specific political outcome.

## Pre-election behavior

- Stocks tend to generate lower returns in the months leading up to midterms
  (Capital Group research)
- Three of the worst midterm years (1990, 2002, 2022) all bottomed between
  October 9 and October 12 (Capital Group)
- The S&P 500 historically gains little ground until shortly before the vote,
  then rallies into year-end

Source: Capital Group, "Midterm elections and markets: 5 charts" (2024)
